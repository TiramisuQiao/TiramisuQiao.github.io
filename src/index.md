## About

I’m **Junqiao Wang (王俊桥)**, a researcher interested in making artificial intelligence useful in **healthcare and biomedicine**. My work explores medical agents, multimodal learning, and efficient large models.

I received my bachelor’s degree in **Computer Science and Technology** from **Sichuan University** in **June 2026**. During my senior year, I interned at the **Shanghai AI Laboratory**, working on medical agents in the Medical Foundation Model Evaluation Group under the mentorship of **Dr. Rongzhao Zhang**. In **September 2026**, I began my doctoral studies in **Electronic Information** at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**, advised by **Prof. Lichi Zhang**, with expected graduation in **2031**.


I am interested in bringing AI to real-world clinical and scientific problems, with a long-term goal of making better healthcare more accessible.

I welcome conversations about medical AI, research collaborations, and open-source projects. The best way to reach me is [tlmsq@outlook.com](mailto:tlmsq@outlook.com).
